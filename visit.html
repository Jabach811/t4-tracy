Our story.

T4 started in Taiwan in 2004 with a simple idea: a cup of "tea for you." We opened the Tracy shop in February 2016, brewing every drink from real tea leaves — never powder — and frying the street snacks we grew up on.

Come in, slow down, and stay a while.
